# About Me

## Cybersecurity Graduate with a Software Engineering Foundation

I am a cybersecurity graduate based in Adelaide, South Australia, with a Master of Information Technology (Cyber Security) from the University of South Australia and a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Software Engineering from the University of Westminster.

My background combines software engineering, cybersecurity, stakeholder engagement, and problem solving. Throughout my career, I have worked across software development, technical consulting, cybersecurity assessment, governance, infrastructure security, and security improvement initiatives.

I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying risks, and helping organisations implement practical solutions that improve security without unnecessarily disrupting business operations.

---

## My Journey into Cybersecurity

I began my career in software engineering, working across front-end development, web technologies, and enterprise software environments.

These roles gave me experience in:

* Software development
* Requirements gathering
* Client engagement
* Technical problem solving
* System design thinking
* Stakeholder communication

While working in technology, I became increasingly interested in cybersecurity and the challenges organisations face in protecting systems, data, and people.

This led me to pursue postgraduate studies in cybersecurity, where I developed knowledge across:

* Cyber Risk Management

## Practical Cybersecurity Experience

One of the most valuable experiences in my cybersecurity journey has been my ongoing volunteering work with the National Railway Museum.

The project involved working alongside museum stakeholders to assess existing infrastructure, identify security improvement opportunities, document risks, and develop practical security recommendations suitable for a resource-constrained non-profit environment:

* Cybersecurity assessment
* Asset discovery
* Infrastructure analysis
* Network segmentation planning
* MFA assessment
* Governance development
* Security awareness
* Stakeholder engagement

The experience has allowed me to apply cybersecurity concepts in a real operational environment while working with stakeholders, technical teams, and organisational constraints.
